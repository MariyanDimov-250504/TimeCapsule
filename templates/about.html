About TimeCapsule

Preserving Memories for the Future

TimeCapsule is a digital platform that allows you to create virtual time capsules - collections of messages, photos, and memories that are sealed until a future date of your choosing.

Our Mission

We believe that meaningful moments deserve to be preserved and rediscovered. Whether it's a letter to your future self, memories of a special occasion, or hopes and dreams for tomorrow, TimeCapsule helps you capture and protect what matters most.
